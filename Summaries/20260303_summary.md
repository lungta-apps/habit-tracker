# Session Summary - March 3, 2026

## Overview

Expanded the habit color palette from 8 to 16 colors, going through two iterations to arrive at a palette where all colors are clearly visually distinct from one another.

## Feature Implemented

### Expanded Color Palette (16 colors)

Users now have 16 color options when creating or editing a habit. The color picker grid is 4 columns × 4 rows.

**First attempt (rejected):** Added 8 pastel versions of the existing 8 colors (e.g., sky-400 alongside blue-500, violet-400 alongside purple-500). These were too visually similar — user identified 4 nearly-indistinguishable pairs: violet≈purple, rose≈pink, amber≈yellow, emerald≈green.

**Final palette:** Solved by combining two strategies:
1. Use distinct named hues (not just shade variants of the same hue) — e.g., replaced `violet` with `fuchsia` (magenta), a genuinely different hue from purple.
2. For hues that are adjacent on the color wheel, use dramatically different shade levels to create clear brightness contrast.

**The 16 colors (in picker order, 4 rows):**

| Row | Colors |
|-----|--------|
| Reds & oranges | `red-500`, `rose-300` (pale), `orange-500`, `amber-600` (dark gold) |
| Yellows & greens | `yellow-400` (bright), `lime-500`, `green-500`, `emerald-700` (dark forest) |
| Teals & blues | `teal-500`, `cyan-500`, `sky-300` (pale), `blue-500` |
| Purples & pinks | `indigo-600` (dark), `purple-500`, `fuchsia-500`, `pink-500` |
